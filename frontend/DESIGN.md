### Color Palette

This document serves as a shared reference point for the team to keep the site's design consistent, and contains the color hash codes and their purposes.

|color sample|hash code|name|purpose|
|-|-|-|-|
|!\[#F7F4EC](https://placehold.co/15x15/F7F4EC/F7F4EC.png)|`#F7F4EC`|background/primary|Main background color and for light-colored objects.|
|!\[#263A32](https://placehold.co/15x15/263A32/263A32.png)|`#263A32`|background/secondary|Secondary background color for the header, footer, navigation and the dark background variant.|
|!\[#111F19](https://placehold.co/15x15/111F19/111F19.png)|`#111F19`|text/primary|Dark color for text and icons on a light background.|
|!\[#FFFDFA](https://placehold.co/15x15/FFFDFA/FFFDFA.png)|`#FFFDFA`|text/inverse|Light color for text and icons on a dark background, and also the reference white in the design.|
|!\[#C0B8A9](https://placehold.co/15x15/C0B8A9/C0B8A9.png)|`#C0B8A9`|accent/grey|Neutral grey; used for buttons, borders, subtitles, helper text and dividers.|
|!\[#7A8A7A](https://placehold.co/15x15/7A8A7A/7A8A7A.png)|`#7A8A7A`|accent/green|Neutral green accent color; used as fill for some icons and sections.|
|!\[#6E896E](https://placehold.co/15x15/6E896E/6E896E.png)|`#6E896E`|button/primary|More vibrant green for selected buttons and highlighted sections, such as the category tag in a post.|
|!\[#E5C98B](https://placehold.co/15x15/E5C98B/E5C98B.png)|`#E5C98B`|button/secondary|Yellow intended for highlighting more important buttons.|
